# ProvinceTacticalFacilityTiles decode — bank $31 $9BA4-$9C57

Formatted data: [docs/province_tactical_facility_tiles.csv](../docs/province_tactical_facility_tiles.csv),
[docs/province_tactical_facility_tiles.md](../docs/province_tactical_facility_tiles.md)
(regenerate: `python tools/extract_tactical_facility_tiles.py`).
Annotated rows: `asm/banks/data_11_officer.asm` label `ProvinceTacticalFacilityTiles`
(generated by `tools/gen_data_bank_asm.py`, verified byte-exact with
`make verifydata`).

## Layout

30 rows x 6 bytes, one row per province id $050E: three ordered (X,Y) tile
pairs. A pair is unused when its X byte has bit7 set (all readers BMI/BPL on
it; the authored padding is `$FF $FF`). Coordinates are tiles of the tactical
battlefield (observed X $01-$1D, Y $01-$13). Neither table ever uses tile row
$0F: that is the province map's blank ribbon gap, the row `@GetTileTerrain`
indexes at y = 15, so a Y of $10 or more addresses authored map row `Y - 1` —
the same skipping the bank $06 deployment pools and the march readers show
(`GetOrderedDestination` subtracts 1 from >=$10).

## Marker positions vs the map stamps

`@DrawCityTargetMarker` ($D296) scales a pair by 16 and draws `@CityMarkerOam`
there: the terrain byte under the cell never enters, so **the table is where the
town is**. The `$79`/`$7A`/`$7B` stamps in the crops are the designer's notes for
the same cells (they only decide the terrain *id*, and ids 6/7/8 are clamped to
Plain by `GetTileTerrainClamped`). The authored data agrees on 55 of the 67
pairs; 12 pairs sit on ordinary terrain (10 of them in provinces 9 西平关, 15
建业, 16 扬州, 26 成都, whose maps stamp nothing but the castle) and 2 stamps
have no pair, one row below theirs: province 12 洛阳 pair (19,17) vs `$7B`
(19,18), province 21 荆州 pair (5,10) vs `$79` (5,11). `tools/check_terrain_pipeline.py`
section 7/9 asserts the ledger, and that no pair ever lands on a `$61` castle cell.

## Slot semantics — fixed facility order

The battlefield is read as four 16x16 quadrants (`GetTileTerrain` prg_08_09_tactical_war
$B6EF, `TileTerrainTable` $B74B: tile bytes $61/$62/$63 = terrain 6/7/8, the
special settlement tiles clamped to plain by the AI wrapper). The table's
three slots are the facility tiles of the province map, in a constant order —
proved directly by the arrival routing:
`MoveState_CheckDest`/`MoveState_CheckDest_Found` (prg_0c_0d_tactical_commands
$A4DD/$A508) stores the matched slot index into $0470 and enters phase $09;
`State9_RouteNextAction` (prg_08_09_tactical_war $C7C8) dispatches on exactly that byte:

| slot | offset | route_mode $0470 | facility | further evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | +0/+1 | $00 -> State0_ShowActionPanel $C0D5 | 民家 House (rice market) | rice-buy panel State0/1 ($C0D5/$C1EB, uses ProvinceRiceTradeRateTable); `Action_BuyRice` $A329 marches AI officers here |
| 1 | +2/+3 | $01 -> State5_ArmoryGridSetup $C4B8 | 武器屋 Armory | armory grid/purchase State5-7 ($C4B8/$C543/$C7AE, `ArmoryGridSetTable[$050E]`) |
| 2 | +4/+5 | else -> State3_CheckVitalityDeficit $C3E3 | 病院 Hospital | vitality-vs-ROM-record check -> State4_HospitalHeal $C3E9 (50 gold); `Action_RestoreHP` $A507 marches to entry +4/+5 |

Caveat: the House slot is **not** the strategy-layer 商店 flag
(`docs/rice_prices.csv` has_market): 青州 (id 3) has a market but no House
tile, 襄阳 (id 22) has no market yet uses the Hospital slot. The tactical-map
facility set is authored independently from the strategy town facilities.

## Readers

Stride-6 (correct row addressing, `$050E *3` then `*2`):

* `DrawStratagemTargetMarkers` prg_08_09_tactical_war $D24D-$D295 — while a battle command
  $0500 < $0C is pending, draws a 16x16 city-target marker
  (`@CityMarkerOam` tiles $3C-$3F) at pixel `(X*16, Y*16)` for each used pair,
  plus `@ProvinceMarkerOam` at the 4-byte row of `ProvincePixelPositionTable`
  $9D58 (pixel Y then pixel X, 16-bit). That table is a tactical position: its
  cell is each province's single $61 castle tile, 30/30 under the row rule above
  (its largest stored pixel, X $0180, is tile 24 of 32 — the "outside the
  battlefield, so strategy-layer space" reading came from swapping Y and X).
  `@CheckPositionHitsCityTile` $D337 suppresses a marker when the position is
  already occupied (battlefield unit coords $0600/$0614, 20 slots) unless
  $005E bit6 is set.
* `MoveState_CheckDest` prg_0c_0d_tactical_commands $A4DD-$A502 — scans the 3 pairs of
  row $050E against the moved officer's destination ($10,$11) loaded from
  $0600/$0614; a match stores the **slot index in X** (`STX` into $0470,
  the action-selector work byte) and enters phase $09 (war action panel,
  `WarTownActionDispatch` — whose states include the rice-buy panel
  State0/1 $C0D5/$C1EB, the hospital heal State4 $C3E9 (50 gold,
  vitality clamped at the ROM base record) and the armory grid
  State5-7 $C4B8. This is the direct
  tile-slot-to-facility link.

Stride-3 (legacy/sliding — the war AI indexes `$050E*3` as if rows were
3-byte entries, so apart from BuyRice it reads across row boundaries; kept as
raw evidence, never "fixed"):

* `Action_BuyRice` $A329 (+0/+1) and its arrival compare $A38A (+0/+1) — own
  row's House pair exactly.
* `Action_RestoreHP` $A507 (+4/+5) and arrival compare $A56E (+4/+5) — lands
  on the **next** province's House pair; the last province reads $FF and the
  action aborts.
* `WarCasualtyResolution` Phases 5/6 $CA5F/$CAC2 — presence tests only:
  byte at +0 != $FF (own row's House X) gates the "reinforcement available"
  morale path (gold >= 100), +4 != $FF (next row's House X) the second
  check (gold >= 50).
