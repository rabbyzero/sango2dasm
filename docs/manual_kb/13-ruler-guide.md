## Rulers covered

Each entry below summarizes the manual's profile + strategy text. Exact stat
numbers are approximate (read from dense tables); verify against the scans.

### 劉備 (Liu Bei) — p. 55
- Base: 并州 (facilities 武/病/商).
- Profile: Han imperial bloodline; weak physically but great human charm,
  attracting elite generals; turns weakness into strength.
- Strategy: few but sharp generals; prioritize defense and development, grow
  generals fast; watch for gaps in defense. Zhuge Liang is exceptionally
  talented.
- Sample roster (并州): 劉備 78/65/71/99/—/1/平; 関羽 97/84/97/88/99/2/水;
  張飛 99/20/98/35/98/2/山.

### 孫策 (Sun Ce) — p. 56
- Bases: 建業, 江夏, 揚州, 建安.
- Profile: succeeded his father, cut through enemies; allied with Zhou Yu;
  laid the groundwork for Wu, succeeded by brother Sun Quan.
- Strategy: high military ability; naval power relatively safe; defend
  coasts vs 江夏; main challenge is nurturing officers in 揚州.

### 馬騰 (Ma Teng) — p. 57
- Bases: 涼州, 西平関, 安定.
- Profile: consolidated the western frontier, strong support among troops and
  people; later undone by Cao Cao's scheme; legacy overshadowed by Ma Chao.
- Strategy: lower development than rivals; needs wise generals beyond Han Sui;
  few elite generals — talent discovery is urgent.
- Sample roster: 馬騰, 韓遂, 侯選, 成宜, 程銀, 張横, 趙月 (山/平 mix).

### 董卓 (Dong Zhuo) — p. 58
- Bases: 洛陽, 長安.
- Profile: ruthless overlord who overstepped morals, yet strong at building
  coalitions.
- Strategy: formidable but vulnerable to betrayal; exploit low-loyalty
  characters and internal divisions.
- Sample roster (洛陽/長安): 董卓, 李儒, 呂布 (体99/知17/武99/忠30/山),
  許褚(?), 張遼(?), 賈詡(?), 李傕, 郭汜, 華雄, 樊稠.

### 曹操 (Cao Cao) — p. 59
- Bases: 兗州, 豫州.
- Profile: also called a tyrant, but won hearts through charisma and strategy;
  unified the north under Wei.
- Strategy: weak western flank (Xuzhou area); inner circle talented but none
  match his strategic depth.
- Sample roster: 曹操 82/89/84/97/—/2/山; 荀彧(?), 夏侯惇 94/60/89/68/92/2/山,
  曹仁, 夏侯淵, 曹洪, 楽進(?).

### 袁紹 (Yuan Shao) — p. 60
- Bases: 冀州, 幽州, 青州.
- Profile: prestigious family, righteous and ambitious; failed through
  overconfidence and poor coordination.
- Strategy: secure safe zones and fortify borders; forces are scattered and
  lack cohesion.
- Sample roster: 袁紹, 顔良 91/37/89/52/87/2/山, 文醜 92/31/90/50/83/2/山,
  田豊 40/93/35/89/90/2/平, 高覧(?), 郭図, 袁譚, 審配(?), 沮授(?).

### 劉璋 (Liu Zhang) — p. 61
- Bases: 成都, 漢中 (and more).
- Profile: values the people's welfare; gentle, non-confrontational; hesitant
  against threats like Cao Cao.
- Strategy: strengths in virtue, numbers, geography, morale; eastern
  territories vulnerable to early invasion — fortify.
- Sample roster: 劉璋, 黄権(?), 黄忠(?), 雷同(?), 厳顔 85/66/86/86/86/3/山,
  呉懿, 張松 38/90/19/59/73/1/平, 法正 61/92/33/82/72/2/平, 孟達.

> Entries marked (?) are names read from dense tables where the scan text was
