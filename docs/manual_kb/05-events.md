# 05 — Events イベント (pp. 28–29)

Source: scan 16 (left page). As months pass, these events may occur:

| Event | Japanese | Reading | Months | Effect |
|-------|----------|---------|--------|--------|
| Tax collection | 税金の徴収 | Zeikin no Choushuu | 1–3 | Gold increases according to 産業, 人口, and 統治度 |
| Flood | 洪水 | Kouzui | 4–6 | Damages 人口/米/産業 etc.; lowers 統治度 and 防災 |
| Drought | 日照り | Hideri | 7–10 | Damages 人口/兵数/土地; lowers 統治度 and 防災 |
| Rice harvest | 米の収穫 | Kome no Shuukaku | 11–12 | Rice increases according to 土地, 人口, and 統治度 |

Notes:
- Damage from floods/droughts is mitigated by the country's 防災 value
  (raise it with the 防災 command).
- Other possible happenings mentioned in the summary (p. 11): peasant uprisings
  and rebellions when 統治度 is low.
