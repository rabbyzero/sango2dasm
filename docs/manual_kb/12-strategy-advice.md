# 12 — Strategic Advice 戦略アドバイス (pp. 50–53)

Sources: scans 27–28. Playbook-style guidance; useful for understanding the
AI priorities the game may implement.

## ① 国力の充実 (strengthen national power, p. 50)

Maintain at least minimum development: 土地開墾 / 産業発展 / 町の開発 and
balanced funding allocation.

## ② 災害の防止 (prevent disasters, p. 50)

Invest in 防災 early using castle commands (防災, 与える to the people);
neglect invites flood/drought damage and unrest.

## ③ 人材の発掘 (discover talent, pp. 50–51)

Six ways to gain generals:
1. 城コマンド「情報集め」— find hidden high-INT/high-virtue generals.
2. 城コマンド「策略」— 引き抜き targets low-loyalty generals; 離間 weakens
   loyal-but-useful enemy staff.
3. 計略「籠絡」— convert enemy generals in battle (beware double agents;
   capture with 包囲 instead).
4. Capture prisoners in 戦闘モード — effective when their loyalty is high.
5. Force surrender in 一騎討ち via 戦術「説得」.
6. Wait for children to mature (成人を待つ) — appointing them too early fails.

## ④ 武将の育成 (develop generals, p. 52)

1. **忠誠度のアップ** — keep loyalty ≥ 70; low loyalty causes defection and
   reduced effectiveness.
2. **智将を育てよう** — cultivate high-intelligence generals for commands.
3. **闘将を育てよう** — cultivate fighters (武力 60–70 balanced with INT);
   pair them with wise generals.

## ⑤ 軍備の強化 (strengthen the military, p. 53)

1. **徴兵** — conscription fills infantry → archers → cavalry in order;
   batches up to 1000; keep refilling (200–300 per run is typical).
2. **兵力の配備** — after 1–2 years, mass strong defensive/offensive
   generals; keep high-loyalty, high-INT generals in safe rear areas.
3. **出陣** — armies of 3–4 generals with ~1000 men each; avoid
   over-extension; value mobility.

Core principle (callout box): 「安全地帯では金や人材を確保し、前線へ送り込む。」
— secure gold and talent in safe zones, push them to the front.
