# 11 — General Level Up 武将のレベルアップ (pp. 46–47)

Source: scan 25.

## Conditions (レベルアップの条件)

- Generals gain **経験値 (experience)** by defeating enemies in war
  (≈ 1 point per enemy soldier defeated).
- Defeating an enemy general grants bonus points.
- Experience is tracked on the general's data; a level-up occurs at each
  100-point threshold (displayed in units of 100).

## Effects (レベルアップの効果)

1. **武力アップ** — combat power rises with level (caps: no further increase
   once above 90).
2. **兵の編成が有利に** — better troop composition: higher-level generals
   field more cavalry (see composition table in
   [06-reference-tables.md](06-reference-tables.md)).
3. **武器と防具の買い替え** — via 武器屋, upgraded weapons/armor raise
   attack/defense.

Summary box: 知力 unlocks 計略/戦術 usage, 武力 drives combat results and
experience gain, equipment upgrades raise attack/defense.
